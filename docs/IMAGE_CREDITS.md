# Image credits

Every photo under `client/public/images/` is a free-to-use stock or openly licensed photograph. The doctors,
hospitals and clinics in the demo data are **fictional**: the pictures only illustrate them, and the people and
buildings shown have no connection to the names, qualifications or addresses on the website.

- **Pexels and Unsplash** photos are free to use without attribution (see their licences); the page is linked for reference.
- **Wikimedia Commons** photos need attribution, which is given below. The licence is shown in the table
  (CC0 needs no credit; CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0 requires credit and sharing changes under the same licence:
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>).
- **Changes made:** `hero/hero-clinic.jpg` was widened on the left with a blurred, mirrored copy of the wall so the headline has room.
  Every photo was cropped and resized (people to a head-and-shoulders portrait, buildings to a
  wide banner) and saved as an optimised JPEG. On two building photos a legible hospital name was painted out
  (`dhanmondi-family-health-clinic.jpg`, `greenline-family-clinic.jpg`) so the picture does not contradict the facility's name.

The images were chosen to look like the people and places of Bangladesh. Stock libraries rarely state nationality,
so "Bangladeshi" here means shot in Bangladesh where the photo says so, otherwise South Asian in appearance.

## Replacing a photo

Put the new JPEG at the same path (doctors 640×800, facilities 1000×400, a face centred in the upper half), then add its
source and licence here. `npm run seed:images` (re)applies the paths to the database; a doctor's own uploaded profile
photo always takes precedence over the demo photo.
